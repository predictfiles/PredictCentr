# PredictCentr — Project Context

## What this is
A news-led prediction market odds comparison site (predictcentr.com) — like Oddschecker, but for prediction markets (Kalshi, Polymarket). Unlike competitors (e.g. iPredicta), the news/context around each market is the main selling point, not just odds comparison. Targets casual/new prediction market bettors, not seasoned traders — simple wording, not a "trading platform" look.

## Who's building it
- Owain Flanders — news editor for a gambling-related news website (that's the "unfair advantage" here: strong news/content instincts, not a trader's mindset).
- Not technical, no budget. Building collaboratively with free tools: GitHub, Vercel, Claude Code.
